01 — Round one

Write a CV. No help.

Ten minutes. No guidance, no examples, no rules — just the boxes. Write whatever you think belongs in a CV. It will probably be weak, and that is the entire point: this is the version we measure the second one against.

This gets locked

When you finish round one it is saved and you cannot edit it again. Do not try to make it good. A dishonest version 1 destroys your own best evidence — the same way quietly improving your first logo does.

02 — The 20 seconds

What a recruiter saw

Your version 1, checked against the things people actually scan for. These are not opinions about your writing — they are mechanical checks, and every one of them is something a real reviewer notices in the first few seconds.

03 — What works

Nine rules that do the work

Not style preferences. These are the things that decide whether a games studio reads past your header.

04 — Build it properly

Version two

Same boxes, with coaching attached and a live preview. The strength meter uses the same checks that judged version 1, so you can watch it climb.

Use the same colour as your logo and website. One identity across everything is what the unit is marked on.

05 — Export

Get it out of here

Nothing here is stored on a server. Export it, keep it, and put both versions in your evidence.

1. Save as PDF

Print, then choose Save as PDF as the destination. Name it Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf. Never send a Word file — it reflows on someone else's machine.

2. Download your data

A small file with both versions in it. Keep it — you will want to edit this CV for every application you make.

3. Put it in your evidence

Version 1, version 2, and a paragraph saying what changed and why. That paragraph is worth more marks than the CV itself.

Before you call it done

The write-up is the assessed part

Open your reflective write-up and answer these: which checks did version 1 fail, which one surprised you, what did you change because of it, and what did you choose not to change and why. That last one is the Distinction question.
